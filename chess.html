Chess Rules & Controls

Standard Chess Rules: Complete official FIDE laws are supported:

  • Pawns: Move 1 square forward (or 2 on initial move), capture 1 square diagonally.
  • En Passant: Capture an opposing pawn that advances two squares past yours.
  • Pawn Promotion: Promote to Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight upon reaching the opposite back rank.
  • Castling: Kingside (O-O) and Queenside (O-O-O) when the path is clear and unattacked.
  • Check & Checkmate: Cannot move into check. Checkmate ends the game.
  • Draws: Stalemate, 50-move rule, and insufficient material automatically detected.

Legal Moves Toggle: Press [L] or use the switch to toggle destination and capture guides on or off.

Keyboard Shortcuts:
• [L]: Toggle Legal Move Guides
• [F]: Flip Board Perspective
• [U]: Undo Move
• [M]: Mute Audio
