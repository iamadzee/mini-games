GAME 01 · TACTICAL ARENA SHOOTER

Ricochet: Chamber Maze

A high-velocity indoor duel where missed blaster shots ricochet up to three times off steel walls. Read angles, bait reloads, and seal doors.

Objective & Victory

Eliminate your rival by tagging them with bouncing laser rounds or baiting them into laser traps and timed chamber doors. The first player to reach the target score wins the match.

Controls

Player One (Cyan)
Move & Aim: WASD
Fire: Space
Dash: Shift
Seal Door: E
Player Two (Coral)
Move & Aim: ↑←↓→
Fire: Enter
Dash: RShift
Seal Door: RCtrl

Mobile: On-screen dual thumbsticks control movement and firing angles.

Core Mechanics

  • Triple Bounce: Laser bolts ricochet up to 3 times before burning out. Be careful — your own bouncing bolts can hit you!
  • 3-Round Magazine: You carry 3 shots. Once empty, your blaster enters a short reload cooldown. Pick up glowing ammo crates to reload immediately.
  • Dash I-Frames: Dashing grants brief invulnerability frames, allowing you to slide through flying bolts unscathed.
  • Chamber Security Gates: Pressing Seal near a doorway shuts the barrier, cutting off retreat paths or deflecting incoming bolts.

Tactical Pro Tips

  • Fire at 45-degree angles into corridor intersections to fill the choke points with crisscrossing hazards.
  • When your opponent exhausts their 3rd shot, immediately dash in and finish them while they are locked in their reload cycle.

GAME 02 · PRECISION COMBAT DUEL

Five Arrows: Parry Duel

A tense 1v1 standoff where every draw is slow, arrows are strictly limited, and a single well-timed parry turns the tide of battle.

Objective & Victory

Deplete your opponent's health bar across multiple rounds through pinpoint archery, parry timing, and close-quarters blade craft.

Controls

Player One (Cyan)
Move: A D · Jump / Duck: W / S
Slash: F
Shield / Parry: G
Draw Bow: R (Hold to nock, release to shoot; W/S aims up/down)
Player Two (Coral)
Move: ← → · Jump / Duck: ↑ / ↓
Slash: .
Shield / Parry: /
Draw Bow: , (Hold to nock, release to shoot; ↑/↓ aims up/down)

Core Mechanics

  • Five Arrows Only: Each fighter starts with exactly 5 arrows. There are no refills. When arrows run out, it becomes a pure sword duel.
  • The Parry Window: Raising your shield just as an arrow arrives deflects the shot. Parrying a sword slash staggers the attacker with a heavy posture break.
  • Melee Interrupt: Hitting an opponent with a sword slash immediately cancels their bow draw and inflicts damage.
  • Collapsible Platforms: Heavy downward slashes and slam landings fracture the cardboard tiers, dropping combatants to lower ground.

Tactical Pro Tips

  • Duck under high arrows instead of gambling on a mistimed parry.
  • Bait your opponent into raising their shield with a fake bow draw, then sprint in with a leaping sword strike during their recovery.

GAME 03 · TIMELESS STRATEGY

Chess: The Board of Kings

The classic game of tactical foresight, positioning, and king safety on an 8×8 board. Full standard international rules with move assist.

Objective & Victory

Trap the enemy King in checkmate — an inescapable threat where the King has no legal squares to flee, cannot block the attack, and cannot capture the attacker.

Controls & Interface

Click or tap any piece to reveal all legal destination squares (green rings) and potential captures (red rings). Click the highlighted square to complete the move. Built-in assistance highlights checks and captures, and the algebraic ledger tracks every move.

Special Rules

  • Castling: If your King and a Rook haven't moved yet and all intervening squares are clear and safe, move the King two squares toward the Rook to castle.
  • En Passant: If an opponent's pawn advances two squares past your pawn on the 5th rank, you can capture it diagonally on your immediate next turn.
  • Pawn Promotion: Advancing a pawn to the 8th rank permits promoting it to a Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight.
  • Stalemate: If the active player has no legal moves and their King is not in check, the match ends in a draw.

Tactical Pro Tips

  • Control the central squares (d4, d5, e4, e5) early with pawns and knights to restrict enemy mobility.
  • Castle early to tuck your King safely behind pawns and connect your rooks along the back rank.

GAME 04 · KINETIC SPORTS DUEL

Cyber Puck: Neon Air Hockey

High-octane neon air hockey powered by sub-pixel 2D collision physics. Zero friction, instant paddle impulse transfer, bank shots off cushions and 45° corner bevels, and 5 game-altering power-up capsules!

The Match Objective

  • 🥅 First to 10 Points: Slap the puck across the center boundary line and blast it into the opposing goal pocket. Each goal awards 1 point.
  • 🔥 Infinite Flame Comet Ball: The puck blazes with permanent fiery plasma momentum, maintaining thrilling high speeds without slowing down!
  • 🤖 Agile AI Rivals: The Medium (Pro) AI maneuvers horizontally and vertically across its defensive half, aggressively challenging passing lanes and driving into slaps.
  • ⚡ Game Point System: When a player reaches 9 points, high-tension Game Point activates. At 9-9 Deuce, sudden death triggers — next goal wins!
  • 🚫 Center Boundary: You cannot cross into the opponent's half of the rink. Defend your zone and snipe open angles.

The 5 Arena Power-Up Orbs

  • 💥 Supernova Warp: Unleashes an explosive warp speed burst (30+ MPH) and concussive shockwave!
  • 🛡️ Laser Barrier: Deploys an impenetrable forcefield over your goal mouth for 8 seconds.
  • ⚡ Multi-Puck: Triplicates the puck into three simultaneous projectiles for pure arcade mayhem!
  • 🔨 Titan Mallet: Expands your mallet paddle radius by +50% for 8 seconds of unstoppable coverage.
  • ❄️ EMP Shockwave: Freezes your opponent's mallet movement dead in its tracks for 1.2 seconds!

Controls & Input Methods

  • 🎮 Player 1 (Cyan / Left): Steer with W, A, S, D or Mouse · Power-Slap with Space / F or Left-Click.
  • 🕹️ Player 2 (Magenta / Right in 2P): Steer with Arrow Keys (↑, ↓, ←, →) · Power-Slap with Enter / Numpad 0.
  • 📱 Mobile & Tablet: Touch & drag to steer your mallet with real kinetic momentum! Tap screen or use the on-screen [⚡ SLAP] button to strike. In 2P Local mode, enjoy simultaneous split-screen multi-touch (P1 left, P2 right).
  • 🛡️ 45° Corner Bevels: Angled tournament corner bevels deflect pucks outward into open ice with exact normal reflection.
