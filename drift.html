Hyper Drift: Circuit Directives

The Race: Complete 3 Laps on the neon championship circuit. Out-drift your rival, cut tight apexes, and unleash combat abilities to claim the checkered flag!

🕹️ Desktop Controls:
• Player 1 (Cyan): Drive with W, A, S, D. Hold Shift / Space while turning to initiate a Power Drift. Tap E / F to fire your active Ability.
• Player 2 (Magenta in 2P Split): Drive with Arrow Keys (↑, ↓, ←, →). Hold Numpad 0 / R-Shift to Drift. Tap Enter / R-Ctrl for Abilities.

📱 Mobile Controls:
• Use on-screen touch buttons for Steering, Gas, Drift, and Abilities. In 2P Split Mode on tablets, both racers play side-by-side with split-screen cameras!

⚡ Drift & Combat Mechanics:
• Sparks & Mini-Turbo: The longer you hold a drift around a bend, the higher your sparks charge: Blue (Stage 1) → Orange (Stage 2) → Purple (Ultra). Release drift to launch forward with a massive mini-turbo surge!
• Persistent Skid Marks: Burning rubber permanently marks the track so you can read your racing lines lap after lap.
• Track Boundaries: Stay on the dark asphalt! Skimming curbs is fine, but running into grass or sand slows your wheels down.
