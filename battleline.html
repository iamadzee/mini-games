Field Manual

Rules of Warfare: 30,000 Soldiers

The Battle: Both armies bring exactly 30,000 soldiers to the field, divided into 6 units (total 60,000 soldiers clashing). You have 15,000 infantry, 10,000 cavalry, and 5,000 archers.

Victory Conditions: Destroy all 6 enemy regiments (inflict 30,000 casualties), OR hold the central Sun Shrine for 2 consecutive turns.

Tactical Rock-Paper-Scissors:
• 🐎 Cavalry vs 🏹 Archers: Cavalry runs down archers (+50% bonus damage).
• 🛡️ Infantry vs 🐎 Cavalry: Spear walls stop cavalry (+40% bonus damage, 30% less counter damage).
• 🏹 Archers vs 🛡️ Infantry: Longbow volleys pepper infantry from range with ZERO return damage.
• Thunder Charge: Cavalry moving 2 or more tiles before attacking deals +50% shock damage!
• Flank Attacks: Striking an enemy from the side or rear deals +40% bonus casualties.

Battlefield Locations (No Grids):
• 🏛️ Stone Ruins: Impassable fortress walls; blocks troops and arrow lines of sight.
• 🌲 Whispering Forest: Woodland cover; absorbs 25% damage from incoming arrows.
• ⚑ Scout Watchtower: Elevated lookout; gives archers stationed here +1 attack range (Range 2-4).
• ☀️ Sun Shrine: Holy solar altar; control for 2 consecutive turns to win the battle!
