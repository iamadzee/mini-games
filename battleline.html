Cyber Puck: Rules & Kinetic Physics

The Objective: Out-maneuver your rival on the zero-friction air hockey table. Smash the puck into the opponent's goal pocket. The first player to score 10 Goals wins the game! Each goal awards 1 point. When a player reaches 9 points, high-tension Game Point pressure activates!

🕹️ Paddle Controls:
• Player 1 (Cyan / Left): Move with W, A, S, D or Mouse. Power-Slap with Space / F or Left-Click.
• Player 2 (Magenta / Right in 2P): Move with Arrow Keys (↑, ↓, ←, →). Power-Slap with Enter / Numpad 0.
• Mobile / Tablet 2-Player: Independent multi-touch — Player 1 touches the left half, Player 2 touches the right half.
• Center Line Boundary: You cannot cross into the opponent's half of the table.

⚡ Kinetic Bank Shots & Arena Physics:
• 45° Corner Bevels: Angled tournament corner cushions smoothly deflect corner pucks outward into open ice, preventing traps.
• Game Point System: Reaching 9 points initiates Game Point tension. At 9-9 Deuce, sudden-death rules trigger — next goal wins!
